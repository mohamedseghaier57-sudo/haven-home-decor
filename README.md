# Haven Home Decor

Build a modern, professional B2B e-commerce marketplace for photo frames and home décor products, inspired by the business model and usability of platforms like Faire and Action, but with a completely original design and branding.

Main Concept

The website sells photo frames, wall decoration, mirrors, posters, decorative objects, and related home décor products to professional customers such as gift shops, furniture stores, interior designers, concept stores, hotels, and retailers.

The platform should feel like a mix between:

A professional wholesale marketplace

A modern e-commerce store

A clean European home-decor catalog

The design must be elegant, minimal, premium, and easy to navigate.

Target Users

Primary customers:

Retail stores

Interior designers

Decoration shops

Gift shops

Hotels

Furniture stores

Online retailers

Professional buyers

The platform should prioritize wholesale purchasing, bulk quantities, professional pricing, and easy reordering.
